#### Q1. 1 3 5 7 ? 2 4 8 16

- (B) 10/17
- (C) 11/34

**Explanation:** Numerators form series 1,3,5,7,9 (add 2). Denominators form 2,4,8,16,32 (powers of 2). Next term 9/32.
